# Prediction markets

**Scope:** Event contracts and prediction markets — pricing mechanics (e.g. AMM / scoring rules), information aggregation, retail and institutional trading angles, and links to crypto rails where venues like [[wiki/shared/entities/polymarket|Polymarket]] sit.

**Out of scope:** General macro forecasting without a markets mechanism; pure sports betting unless tied to contract design or venue economics.
