# Media automation

**Scope:** Scripted or agent-assisted generation of video, shorts, and related media pipelines (compose, TTS, upload workflows) — especially OSS repos and tooling that sit next to general LLM dev stacks.

**Out of scope:** Prediction markets; pure LLM chat monetization without a media pipeline (see [[wiki/domains/llm-monetization/overview|llm-monetization]]).
