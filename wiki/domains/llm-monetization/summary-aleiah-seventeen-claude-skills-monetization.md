# Aleiah — 17 Claude skills & digital products

**Source:** X Article by [@AleiahLock](https://x.com/AleiahLock/status/2038295590354755683), saved as `raw/inbox/Aleiah on X_ _17 Claude Skills → $312_Day → $10K_Month. Let Me Show You. _ _ X.htm`.

**Note:** Different source from [[wiki/domains/prediction-markets/summary-aleiah-polymarket-quant-playbook-2026|Aleiah’s Polymarket quant playbook]] (separate article). Income figures and marketplace examples are **author claims**. The piece includes a **“Digital Success Blueprint”** CTA — treat as **affiliate / course promotion**.

## Part A — Seventeen “skills” (author)

Framing: sell **repeatable outputs** that remove pain (time, risk, revenue), not “AI writing” generically.

| # | Theme | Examples (compressed) |
|---|--------|------------------------|
| 1–3 | Business writing | Cold-email personalization, support macros, internal SOPs from Slack chaos |
| 4–7 | Research deliverables | Objection mining, pricing-page tear-downs, competitor feature tables, plain-English policy summaries (non-legal) |
| 8–11 | Marketing assets | Landing section rewrites, multi-angle ad ideas, email-sequence **logic**, repurposing pipelines |
| 12–15 | Ops | CRM cleanup/tagging, meeting → actions, JD “reality checks,” vendor comparison matrices |
| 16–17 | Personal services | Resume positioning, decision frameworks |

**What failed (author):** prompt packs, generic chatbots, undifferentiated “AI consultant” offers — “nobody wakes up wanting AI.”

## Part B — Notion templates & Claude Opus (author)

- Argues **Notion** marketplace demand + **near-zero marginal cost** for digital templates; AI lowers build friction.
- Workflow sketch: define product (e.g. debt snowball planner) → structured plan with Claude → build/iterate in Notion / AI-assisted flow.
- Stresses **listing quality**, niching, competition — warns **supply will rise** as AI template creation spreads; “taste” and positioning as moat vs model access.

## Part C — Artifacts & multi-SKU PDFs (author)

- Claude **artifacts** for structured downloadable products (planners, trackers); **triple-layer** pricing (PDF / editable / bundle).
- **Validation:** quick Etsy title search + optional free post in communities before building paid SKU.
