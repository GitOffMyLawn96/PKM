# LLM monetization

**Scope:** Indie and freelance business models built on LLM outputs — “skills,” digital products, marketplace listings, and pricing psychology — treated as **source narratives**, not financial advice.

**Out of scope:** Technical agent harness design (see [[wiki/domains/llm-dev-tooling/overview|llm-dev-tooling]]); prediction-market trading (see [[wiki/domains/prediction-markets/overview|prediction-markets]]).
