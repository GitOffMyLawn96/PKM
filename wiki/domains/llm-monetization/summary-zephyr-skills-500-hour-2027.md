# Zephyr — seven skills worth $500/hr by 2027 (career essay)

**Source:** X Article by [@Zephyr_hg](https://x.com/Zephyr_hg/status/2035651131817234833), saved as `raw/inbox/Zephyr on X_ _The Skills That Will Be Worth $500_Hour in 2027 (That Are Free to Learn Today)_ _ X.htm`.

**Note:** Ends with a **product CTA** (“Mastery Bundle”). Wage projections are **speculative**.

## Seven skill areas (author)

1. **AI system architecture (non-coding)** — Map business processes; design human/AI/automation splits and data flow.
2. **AI training data curation** — Domain-specific datasets as “librarianship” for useful models (not generic generic data).
3. **No-code AI workflows** — Visual orchestration (author names **n8n**, **Make**, **Zapier**) connecting systems to AI steps.
4. **AI output quality control** — Editorial / brand judgment on AI-generated work at scale.
5. **Automation maintenance & optimization** — Auditing and fixing drifting automations (“mechanic” role).
6. **Context engineering** — Rich persistent context so AI is useful without brittle one-off prompts (vs commoditized “prompt engineering”).
7. **AI–human workflow design** — Handoff patterns for hybrid pipelines (speed + quality).

## Thesis

- **Accessibility:** claims no degree, no coding, no paid learning required — but **early mover** advantage vs “commoditized” prompt-only users.
