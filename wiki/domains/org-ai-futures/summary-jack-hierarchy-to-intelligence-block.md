# jack — From Hierarchy to Intelligence (Block)

**Source:** Long-form X Article attributed in the save to [@jack](https://x.com/jack/status/2039003879841362278) (Block / Sequoia framing in text), saved as `raw/inbox/jack on X_ _From Hierarchy to Intelligence_ _ X.htm`.  
**Canonical essay URL (cited in piece):** [block.xyz/inside/from-hierarchy-to-intelligence](https://block.xyz/inside/from-hierarchy-to-intelligence)

## Arc (author)

- **History:** Roman army nested units → Prussian General Staff (“staff vs line”) → railroad org charts (McCallum) → Taylorism → Manhattan Project cross-functional exception → postwar matrix (McKinsey / Shell / GE) → 7-S → tech experiments (Spotify squads, Holacracy, Valve) — all circling **span of control** vs **information speed**.
- **Claim:** hierarchy persisted because no alternative could route information at scale; **AI** may replace coordination layers humans used to provide.

## Block-specific vision (author)

- **Two models:** (1) **company world model** — internal state from remote-first artifacts; (2) **customer world model** — behavior from transaction “honest signal” (Cash App, Square, etc.).
- **Four build layers:** financial **capabilities** (primitives, not products) → **world models** → **intelligence layer** (composes capabilities into timely solutions) → **interfaces** (Square, Cash App, Afterpay, etc. as delivery surfaces).
- **Roadmap inversion:** missing capability detected when composition fails → backlog from reality vs PM speculation.
- **Roles (three):** ICs deep in each layer; **DRIs** on cross-cutting outcomes; **player-coaches** for craft + people growth — **no permanent middle management** in the target state.
- **Edge:** humans where intuition, ethics, novelty, and “feel” matter; system supplies shared context so fewer alignment meetings.

## Critical reading

- This is a **strategic narrative** for one company’s transformation, not a general law of org design.
- “Mini-AGI” / full replacement of management is **aspirational**; implementation risk and labor/regulatory context are barely scoped in the essay.
