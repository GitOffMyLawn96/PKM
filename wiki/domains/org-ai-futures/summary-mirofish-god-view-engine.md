# MiroFish — multi-agent simulation “God View” (X article)

**Source:** X Article (saved HTML) — canonical URL in save: [x.com/k1rallik/status/2032837113247395992](https://x.com/k1rallik/status/2032837113247395992). File: `raw/inbox/BuBBliK on X_ _MiroFish_ The God View Engine_ _ X.htm`.

## Storyline (author)

- **MiroFish:** system that ingests a document, builds a **knowledge graph** (**GraphRAG**), spawns **thousands** of persona-rich agents, and runs a **simulation** where operators can inject shocks (“Fed cuts 50bps”, “CEO resigns”, etc.) and watch the society of agents **re-equilibrate** — branded **“God’s Eye View.”**
- **Creator:** Guo Hangjiang (online “BaiFu”), undergraduate; prior project **BettaFish** (multi-agent public-opinion analyzer) allegedly hit GitHub trending at scale.
- **Patron narrative:** **Chen Tianqiao** (Shanda) and a **~30M yuan (~\$4.1M)** incubation commitment tied to a rapid **10-day** build — framed as validation of a **“super-individual”** thesis (one person shipping what once needed a company).

## Technical claims in the piece

- **Simulation:** **OASIS** ([CAMEL-AI](https://github.com/camel-ai)) for social dynamics; **Zep Cloud** for long-term memory; graph from **GraphRAG**; **AGPL-3.0**; **Docker Compose** deploy.
- **Demos described:** (1) *Dream of the Red Chamber* — character agents and alternate endings; (2) **Fed hike** — retail vs institutional vs analyst reactions and opinion trajectories.
- **Repo cited:** [github.com/666ghj/MiroFish](https://github.com/666ghj/MiroFish)

## Critical reading (article acknowledges)

- **No published benchmarks** vs real-world outcomes; demos are **illustrations**, not accuracy evidence.
- **Cost:** many agents ⇒ heavy **LLM API** spend.
- **Bias:** personas inherit model / data biases; **not** human populations.
