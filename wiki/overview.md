# Overview

This wiki is a **persistent, compounding** layer between you and **raw sources** in `raw/`. The human curates sources and questions; the agent summarizes, cross-links, updates entity and concept pages, and keeps [[wiki/index|index]] current.

## Domains

Unrelated topic areas live in parallel under [[wiki/index#Domains|domains]] so namespaces stay clear:

- [[wiki/domains/crypto-hft/overview|Crypto HFT]] — trading, markets, infrastructure (to be filled from sources).
- [[wiki/domains/space-systems/overview|Space systems]] — orbital mechanics, vehicles, regulations (to be filled from sources).

Link **up** here when a domain touches the big picture; link **sideways** to pages under `wiki/shared/concepts/` and `wiki/shared/entities/` when ideas or named things span domains.

## Shared layer

- **Concepts**: `wiki/shared/concepts/` — abstractions used in more than one domain.
- **Entities**: `wiki/shared/entities/` — people, orgs, products, missions.

## Source of truth

Files under `raw/` are **immutable** for the agent: read only, never edit. The wiki interprets and links to them.
